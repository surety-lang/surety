Signature, in words: `a10n` takes the required argument `string`.

The docstring:

That thing where "internationalization" becomes "i18n", what's it
called? Abbreviation? Oh wait, no: ``a10n``. (It's actually a form
of `numeronym`_.)

>>> a10n('abbreviation')
'a10n'
>>> a10n('internationalization')
'i18n'
>>> a10n('')
''
