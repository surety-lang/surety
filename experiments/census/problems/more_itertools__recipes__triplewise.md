more_itertools.recipes.triplewise (from itertools recipes (more_itertools.recipes) 11.2.1)

Signature, in words: `triplewise` takes the required argument `iterable`.

The docstring:

Return overlapping triplets from *iterable*.

>>> list(triplewise('ABCDE'))
[('A', 'B', 'C'), ('B', 'C', 'D'), ('C', 'D', 'E')]
